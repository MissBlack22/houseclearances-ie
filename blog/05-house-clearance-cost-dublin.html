How Much Does a House Clearance Cost in Dublin?

There's no single fixed price for a house clearance, and any company quoting one figure before seeing what's actually involved is guessing. What a clearance costs depends on a handful of specific factors — here's what they are and how to get a quote that's actually accurate.

What Actually Drives the Price

  • Volume — how much needs to be cleared, roughly speaking, in van loads. A single room is a very different job to a full three-bed house.
  • Access — ground floor with easy van access is quicker than a third-floor apartment with no lift, or a rural property down a long lane.
  • Type of items — bulky furniture, appliances, or anything requiring extra care (electricals, certain waste categories) can affect price.
  • How much sorting is needed — a house that's already tidy and organised is faster to clear than one that needs careful sorting room by room.
  • Timeframe — a same-day or urgent job may cost more than one that can be scheduled flexibly.

How Quotes Are Usually Worked Out

Most genuine clearance companies price based on volume and access rather than a flat hourly or per-item rate, since a job that takes three hours because of a tricky staircase shouldn't cost the same as one that takes forty-five minutes with a driveway right outside the door. We price from photos or a short video sent over WhatsApp for most jobs, which is usually enough to give an accurate free quote without needing a site visit.

Why "Cheapest" Isn't Always Cheapest

A very low quote is sometimes a sign that waste disposal costs haven't been factored in properly — which either means the company is operating at a loss (unlikely to last), or the waste isn't being disposed of through a licensed route, which can leave the property owner exposed if it's ever traced back through flytipping. It's worth asking whether a company is insured and holds a valid Waste Collection Permit before booking on price alone.

Getting the Most Accurate Quote

The more detail you can give upfront — photos or video of each room, roughly how much needs to go, and any access issues (stairs, parking, lift availability) — the more accurate the quote will be, and the less likely there'll be a surprise on the day.
