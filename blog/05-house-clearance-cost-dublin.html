How Much Does a House Clearance Cost in Dublin?

There's no single fixed price for a house clearance, and any company quoting one figure before seeing what's actually involved is guessing. What a clearance costs depends on a handful of specific factors — here's what they are and how to get a quote that's actually accurate.

This page is about how pricing works in Dublin specifically, including the city-centre parking, apartment access and disposal issues that move a quote up or down. For the national picture, see our longer guide to the cost of house clearance in Ireland. For the service itself, see our house clearance page.

What Actually Drives the Price

  • Volume — how much needs to be cleared, roughly speaking, in van loads. A single room is a very different job to a full three-bed house.
  • Access — ground floor with easy van access is quicker than a third-floor apartment with no lift, or a rural property down a long lane.
  • Type of items — bulky furniture, appliances, or anything requiring extra care (electricals, certain waste categories) can affect price.
  • How much sorting is needed — a house that's already tidy and organised is faster to clear than one that needs careful sorting room by room.
  • Timeframe — a same-day or urgent job may cost more than one that can be scheduled flexibly.

The Rest of the Picture: Rooms, Outbuildings and Crew

Beyond those headline factors, a few others quietly shape a quote:

  • Number of rooms and areas. A clearance that covers every room plus the attic, garage and shed takes more time and van space than one or two rooms. Outbuildings are often forgotten when people describe a job, so mention them.
  • Heavy and awkward items. Wardrobes that do not come apart, pianos, safes, gym equipment and large appliances call for more people and more care.
  • Distance to the van. A long carry from the back of a house or garden adds time even when the volume is modest.
  • Number of people and van loads. Larger jobs may need a bigger crew or more than one load to finish in a single visit.
  • Disposal and recycling. Waste has to go through licensed routes, and some materials need separate handling. Items in good condition can be set aside for donation or resale, which can reduce what has to be disposed of.

What Is Different About Dublin

The same house cleared in Dublin and in a rural county can be quoted differently, because of the way the city works. These are not extras added to a bill, they are real differences in how long a job takes:

Parking and Loading

Where there is paid or restricted parking, getting a van close to the door is a job in itself. Dublin City Council allows paid parking spaces to be suspended on application for uses such as set-down and collection, with a number of working days' notice and a daily fee for each space (see the council's page for current details). Not every job needs this, but on a busy street it can matter, and it is worth mentioning when you ask for a quote.

Apartments, Lifts and Building Rules

A third-floor flat with no lift takes longer than a ground-floor house with a driveway, even when there is less in it. Many apartment developments are run by an owners' management company that can set house rules for the building, which may cover moving times, lift use and loading areas. Tell us about these when you ask for a quote. Our apartment clearance page goes through the access questions we ask.

Older Houses

Terraced and period houses often have narrow staircases, tight hallways and small attic hatches. That does not usually change what is removed, but it can change how long it takes. See our pages on attic clearance and garage clearance for what to expect with each.

Why You Cannot Easily Do It Yourself Cheaper

Some people plan to do it themselves with a borrowed van and trips to the recycling centre. Council recycling centres are set up for householders' own domestic use, and some state that commercial waste is not accepted, and that bulky items and certain materials are charged for. Check the council's current rules before you go (for example Ballyogan and Fingal's centres). For a whole house, the time and the repeated trips often outweigh the saving.

How Quotes Are Usually Worked Out

Most genuine clearance companies price based on volume and access rather than a flat hourly or per-item rate, since a job that takes three hours because of a tricky staircase shouldn't cost the same as one that takes forty-five minutes with a driveway right outside the door. We price from photos or a short video sent over WhatsApp for most jobs, which is usually enough to give an accurate free quote without needing a site visit.

Three Examples of How Jobs Differ (Without Inventing Prices)

We do not publish a price list, because the same-sized property can be a very different job. These are descriptions of how jobs differ, not price points:

  • A one-bed flat on the third floor with no lift. Not much in it, but every item goes down the stairs, and parking may be restricted. Time on site is driven by the stairs.
  • A three-bed semi with a driveway and a full attic. Much more in it, but the van is at the door. Time on site is driven by volume, plus the attic hatch.
  • A house with decades of belongings. Volume is high and there is a lot to sort. Time on site is driven by sorting and by what the family wants to keep. See hoarder clearance if the property is very full.

That is why a "price per bedroom" figure can be misleading. It averages over jobs that are not alike.

Why Online Quotes Vary So Much

If you compare quotes, you will see a wide spread, and it usually comes down to assumptions rather than greed:

  • One company has priced a full clearance and another only the main rooms.
  • One has included disposal and another has not, or has left it to be added later.
  • One has assumed easy access and another has allowed for stairs and parking.
  • One has quoted over the phone without seeing anything and is guessing.

Ask each company what its price includes. Our guide to choosing a house clearance company has a list of questions that makes quotes easier to compare.

Why "Cheapest" Isn't Always Cheapest

A very low quote is sometimes a sign that waste disposal costs haven't been factored in properly — which either means the company is operating at a loss (unlikely to last), or the waste isn't being disposed of through a licensed route, which can leave the property owner exposed if it's ever traced back through flytipping. It's worth asking whether a company is insured and holds a valid Waste Collection Permit before booking on price alone. You can look up any collector on the NWCPO permit search.

Getting the Most Accurate Quote

The more detail you can give upfront — photos or video of each room, roughly how much needs to go, and any access issues (stairs, parking, lift availability) — the more accurate the quote will be, and the less likely there'll be a surprise on the day. If a job is more or less than expected once we arrive, we'll always talk to you about it rather than just adding to the bill.

Useful photos include:

  • Each room, from the doorway, so we can see the whole space.
  • Large or heavy items on their own: wardrobes, pianos, appliances.
  • The attic hatch, garage and shed, including the inside.
  • The front of the property and where a van could park.
  • The stairs, hallway or lift, if there are any.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fixed price for a house clearance in Dublin?

No. Two houses of the same size can differ a great deal in volume, access and sorting. We give a specific free quote before anything is booked.

Is the quote final?

The quote is based on what we are told and shown. If the job turns out different once we arrive, we talk to you about it first rather than simply adding to the bill. Ask any company what happens if a job is bigger or smaller than agreed.

Does the price include disposal?

Ask every company this directly, as it is one of the biggest reasons quotes differ. We are a fully insured waste carrier and items are disposed of through licensed routes.

Can I get a quote from photos?

Yes. Photos or a short video of each room, and of any attic, garage or shed, are usually enough for an accurate quote without a visit.

Does clearing a flat cost more than a house?

Not necessarily. A flat usually has less in it but can be slower to clear if there are stairs, no lift or restricted parking. It depends on the building.

Do you charge more for urgent jobs?

A same-day or urgent job may cost more than one that can be scheduled flexibly. Tell us your date and we will say what is realistic.
