House Clearance Before Probate

One of the most common questions we get is whether a house can be cleared before probate is granted. The honest answer is that this depends on the specific estate, and it's a question for the solicitor or executor handling the process, not for a clearance company — we're not in a position to give legal advice on probate timing. What we can say from doing this work regularly is that most families take one of two approaches: securing the property and clearing anything genuinely urgent straight away, or holding off on the fuller clearance until probate is granted or the family has agreed on what happens to the estate.

Either way, we work to whatever timeline the family or executor sets, not the other way round. Attic Clearance in Dublin

Attics are one of the most common things people search for alongside general house clearance, and for good reason — most Dublin attics haven't been properly sorted in years. Boxes from a move that never got unpacked, old decorations, documents, and furniture nobody has room for downstairs tend to build up quietly until the hatch barely opens.

Attic clearance is also genuinely more physical than clearing a normal room — working through a hatch, on a ladder, often with low headroom and no proper flooring in parts of the space. Done carelessly, that's a real safety risk, which is why we treat it as its own job rather than something to rush. See our attic clearance page for how we handle access safely, or our piece on what's actually worth keeping in an attic clearance if you're trying to work out what to do with everything up there before we arrive.

Attic, Basement and Cellar Clearances

Basements and cellars are far less common in Dublin housing than attics — most 20th-century suburban houses simply don't have one. Where they do turn up, it's typically in older Georgian or Victorian properties with a basement or garden-level floor, sometimes used for storage in the same way an attic is elsewhere. If your property does have a basement, cellar, or garden-level room that's built up over the years, it's cleared the same way as any other space — sorted, loaded, and taken away in the same visit as the rest of the house if that's what's needed. Mention it when you get in touch so it's factored into the quote rather than discovered on the day.

Preparing for a House Clearance

You don't need to sort through everything before we arrive — that's part of the job. That said, a few things genuinely help a quote run smoothly and the day itself go faster. Set aside anything you're definitely keeping somewhere separate, so it's obviously not part of the clearance. If specific items need to be found and kept — documents, jewellery, photographs — flag them when you book rather than on the day, so we know to look out for them as we go. And if access is unusual in any way — no parking outside, a narrow lane, a lift that needs booking, a locked storage area — mentioning it upfront means the crew turns up with the right approach rather than working it out on the doorstep.
