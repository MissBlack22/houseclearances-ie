Getting Access Sorted

Swords has a real mix of property types — older terraced housing near the town centre, newer estates further out, and apartment developments with managed parking or restricted access. Whichever applies to your property, it's worth having a think about van access and parking before the day: whether there's a driveway, whether residents' parking permits are needed, or whether an apartment block requires prior notice to a management company. Typical Turnaround
