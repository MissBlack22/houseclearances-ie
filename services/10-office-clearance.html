Office Clearance

Clearing an office is different from clearing a house — desks and filing cabinets instead of furniture, a working business to avoid disrupting, and sometimes IT equipment that needs a bit of care around what's on it. HouseClearances.ie provides office clearance, commercial clearance and business clearout services across Dublin and Leinster, scheduled around your business rather than the other way round.

Common Reasons Businesses Contact Us

  • Office relocation — clearing the old space once the move is done.
  • Downsizing or a refit — removing surplus desks, chairs, and storage.
  • Lease-end clearances — returning a unit to the landlord in an agreed condition.
  • General decluttering — old files, redundant equipment, and furniture nobody's using.

What We Clear

Desks, chairs, filing cabinets, shelving, general office furniture, and old paperwork or files. For IT equipment — old PCs, monitors, servers — let us know at the enquiry stage; we handle the physical removal, but any equipment that may hold company data is your responsibility to wipe or have professionally destroyed before we take it, unless you've arranged that separately.

Minimal Disruption

Most office clearances happen outside normal working hours, or over a weekend, specifically so it doesn't interrupt your team. Tell us your preferred timing when you enquire and we'll work around it.

Responsible Disposal

We're a fully insured waste carrier, and prioritise reuse and recycling ahead of landfill — office furniture in good condition is set aside for donation or resale wherever possible, and we aim for an average recycling rate of up to 95% across everything we clear.

Types of Commercial Space We Clear

"Office clearance" covers a range of workspaces beyond a traditional open-plan office — co-working spaces and serviced offices, reception areas, meeting rooms, small business premises, retail back-offices, and shared workspace units. Whatever the layout, the same process applies: a free quote, a scheduled visit, and a clean handover.

Confidential Waste and Filing

Old paper files and filing cabinets often contain sensitive business or client information. We remove the physical filing cabinets and paperwork as part of a standard office clearance, but if documents need to be securely shredded rather than simply recycled, let us know in advance so we can plan for it — either through your own confidential waste provider beforehand, or by flagging it as part of the quote.

How It Works

  1. Get in touch with the size of the office and roughly what needs clearing.
  2. We provide a free, no-obligation quote, confirming timing around your business hours.
  3. We schedule the clearance for a time that suits you.
  4. We clear the space, sorting furniture and equipment responsibly.
  5. The unit is left ready for handover or its next use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you clear an office outside working hours?

Yes — evening, weekend, and out-of-hours clearances are common for office jobs. Tell us your preferred timing when you enquire.

Do you wipe data from old computers and equipment?

No — we handle the physical removal, but any equipment that may hold company data should be wiped or professionally destroyed beforehand, unless arranged with us separately in advance.

Can you clear a large open-plan office in one visit?

In most cases, yes — for larger offices we'll confirm at the quote stage whether one visit is enough.
