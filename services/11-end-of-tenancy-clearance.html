End of Tenancy Clearance

Every day a rental property sits uncleared between tenants is a day it's not earning rent. HouseClearances.ie provides end of tenancy clearance, rental property clearance and landlord clearout services across Dublin and Leinster, working to landlords' and letting agents' deadlines so the property is back on the market as fast as possible.

Who This Is For

  • Landlords turning a property around between tenants.
  • Letting agents managing clearances across a portfolio of units.
  • Tenants themselves, arranging a clearance as part of moving out and handing back the keys.

What We Clear

Abandoned furniture, rubbish, and general belongings left behind by a previous tenant — full properties or single rooms. We work to get the property to a genuinely lettable standard: cleared, swept, and ready for the next tenant's viewing.

Speed Matters Here

Unlike a standard house clearance, the priority in a tenancy turnaround is almost always the date — a new tenant moving in, a viewing already booked, or an agent needing the keys back. Tell us your deadline when you enquire and we'll confirm what's realistic, including same-week turnarounds where possible.

For Letting Agents Managing Multiple Units

If you're clearing more than one property on a regular basis, we can discuss a standing arrangement rather than a one-off quote every time — a single point of contact, consistent pricing, and priority scheduling around your turnaround dates.

Responsible Disposal

We're a fully insured waste carrier. Furniture and belongings in good condition are set aside for donation or resale wherever possible, and we aim for an average recycling rate of up to 95% rather than sending everything to landfill.

House Shares and HMOs

Shared houses and HMOs (houses in multiple occupation) bring their own complication — several tenants' belongings mixed together, sometimes with different move-out dates. Tell us the situation when you enquire and we'll work out a sensible approach, whether that's one clearance once everyone's out or clearing individual rooms as they empty.

Deposit and Handover Deadlines

We understand that a tenancy clearance is often tied to a hard deadline — a deposit return, a handover inspection, or a new tenant's move-in date already booked. Tell us the date that matters and we'll build the schedule around it rather than fitting you in whenever suits us.

How It Works

  1. Get in touch with the property details and your deadline.
  2. We provide a free, no-obligation quote.
  3. We book the clearance around your turnaround date.
  4. We clear the property, sorting items responsibly.
  5. The property is left lettable — cleared, swept, and ready for viewings.
