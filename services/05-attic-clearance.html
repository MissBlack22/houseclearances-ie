Attic Clearance

Attics collect whatever a household doesn't want to look at but isn't ready to throw out — boxes from a move that never got unpacked, decorations, old documents, furniture nobody has room for downstairs, and things that have simply been up there so long nobody remembers what's in the box. HouseClearances.ie clears attics across Dublin, including the awkward physical side of the job that a lot of people underestimate until they're halfway up a ladder.

Why Attics Are Their Own Job

Unlike most other rooms, an attic clearance usually means working through a hatch, on a ladder, with low headroom, exposed joists, and no proper flooring in parts of the space. It's genuinely awkward and, done carelessly, genuinely unsafe — stepping through a ceiling is a real risk if you're not used to working in that environment. Our crews handle attic access properly, including moving items safely down through the hatch without damaging ceilings, walls, or stairwells on the way.

What Typically Comes Out

  • Boxes of belongings, often unopened for years
  • Old furniture stored out of the way
  • Christmas decorations and seasonal items
  • Documents, books, and paperwork
  • Luggage, sports equipment, and general household overflow
  • Insulation-adjacent debris where it's part of general clearance (not an insulation removal service)

Sorting as We Go

Attics are one of the few spaces where people genuinely aren't sure what's up there. If you want to keep the option of going through boxes before anything is disposed of, tell us in advance and we'll bring items down for you to check rather than clearing everything straight to the van. Otherwise, we sort as standard — items in good condition set aside for donation or resale, the rest recycled or disposed of responsibly.

Responsible Disposal

We're a fully insured waste carrier, and aim for an average recycling rate of up to 95% across everything we handle, prioritising reuse and donation ahead of landfill. Anything genuinely unsuitable for reuse or recycling goes through a licensed waste transfer station.

Frequently Asked Questions

Access is factored into the quote once we know how the attic is reached — a proper staircase and boarded floor is a different job from a pull-down ladder and no flooring. Tell us what you've got and we'll quote accordingly.

Can I look through boxes before you dispose of anything?

Yes — just let us know in advance and we'll bring items down for you to check rather than clearing straight to the van.

Is it safe to clear an attic with no proper flooring?

Our crews are used to working in exactly that kind of space and take the appropriate care — that's part of why this is a dedicated service rather than something we treat as routine as clearing a bedroom.
