Warehouse Clearance

Clearing a warehouse or commercial unit is a different kind of job to a house clearance — bigger volumes, business deadlines attached to it, and usually a lease-end date or handover that isn't moving. HouseClearances.ie handles warehouse and commercial property clearance across Dublin for business closures, relocations, and lease-end clearances, scheduled around your business rather than the other way round.

Common Reasons Businesses Contact Us

  • Closing a business and needing the unit fully cleared and handed back.
  • Relocating and leaving surplus stock, fixtures, or equipment behind.
  • Lease-end clearances, where a unit needs to be returned to the landlord in an agreed condition.
  • General stock or fixture disposal during a refit or downsizing.

What We Clear

General stock, packaging, fixtures, furniture, and accumulated commercial waste. If your clearance involves specific heavy machinery, industrial equipment, or anything requiring specialist plant to move, let us know the detail at the enquiry stage so we can confirm what's realistic and, where needed, be upfront about anything outside what we handle directly.

Scheduling Around Your Business

Lease-end and relocation dates are usually fixed, and we work to them. Tell us your deadline when you get in touch and we'll confirm whether a single visit is enough or whether a larger job needs to be scoped across more than one day, so you're not left guessing close to the handover date.

Responsible Disposal

As with our residential work, we're a fully insured waste carrier and prioritise reuse and recycling over landfill wherever the material allows, aiming for an average recycling rate of up to 95% across what we handle. Genuine commercial waste unsuitable for reuse or recycling is taken to a licensed waste transfer station.

How It Works

  1. Get in touch with the unit size, roughly what needs clearing, and your deadline.
  2. We provide a free quote, confirming timeline and scope.
  3. We schedule the clearance to fit your lease-end or handover date.
  4. We clear the unit, sorting stock, fixtures and waste as we go.
  5. The unit is left ready for handover, inspection, or the next tenant.
