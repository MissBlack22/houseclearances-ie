Garden Waste Clearance

Gardens that have been left to grow for a season — or several — build up more waste than most people expect: cuttings, branches, old decking, overgrown hedging, and whatever's accumulated in the corners over time. HouseClearances.ie provides garden waste clearance, green waste removal and garden clearance services across Dublin and Leinster, whether it's a one-off tidy-up or preparing a property for sale.

What Typically Needs Clearing

  • Grass cuttings, hedge trimmings, and general green waste
  • Fallen branches and overgrown vegetation
  • Old decking, fencing, and garden structures being removed
  • General outdoor clutter — pots, broken furniture, leftover building materials
  • Gardens that have gone untended and need a full clear-out before anything else can happen

Often Tied to a Wider Clearance

Garden waste clearance is frequently booked alongside a full house clearance or shed clearance — particularly for properties being prepared for sale, where an overgrown garden needs sorting alongside the house itself. Mention everything that needs clearing when you get in touch and we'll quote for it as one job.

Responsible Disposal

Green waste is genuinely recyclable, and we treat it that way — composted or recycled wherever possible rather than sent to general landfill. We're a fully insured waste carrier, and aim for an average recycling rate of up to 95% across everything we clear.

Preparing a Property for Sale or Letting

An overgrown garden is one of the first things a buyer or prospective tenant notices, and one of the easiest to fix before photos or viewings. We clear gardens as a stand-alone job specifically for this — cutting back, clearing debris, and leaving the outdoor space presentable without needing a full landscaping job.

Skip Hire Alternative

For a lot of garden clean-ups, hiring a skip means paying for a permit, having it sit on the road for days, and loading it yourself. We do the loading and take everything away in one visit, sorted for recycling rather than dumped as mixed waste — often simpler and no more expensive than a skip once you account for the permit and your own time.
