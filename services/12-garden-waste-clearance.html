Garden Waste Clearance

Gardens that have been left to grow for a season — or several — build up more waste than most people expect: cuttings, branches, old decking, overgrown hedging, and whatever's accumulated in the corners over time. HouseClearances.ie provides garden waste clearance, green waste removal and garden clearance services across Dublin and Leinster, whether it's a one-off tidy-up or preparing a property for sale.

What Typically Needs Clearing

Every overgrown garden is different, but the waste tends to fall into a handful of recognisable categories. Here's what we're most often asked to clear:

  • Green waste and cuttings — grass cuttings, hedge trimmings, leaf fall, and general vegetation from a garden that's had a season or more without proper upkeep.
  • Branches and overgrowth — fallen or cut branches, brambles, and shrubs that have spread well beyond where they were planted.
  • Old decking, fencing, and garden structures — timber that's rotted, panels that have blown down, and structures being taken out rather than repaired.
  • General outdoor clutter — plant pots, broken garden furniture, leftover building materials, and whatever's been dumped at the back of the garden "for later."
  • Neglected gardens needing a full reset — where the garden hasn't been touched in years and needs clearing back to bare ground before anything else — planting, landscaping, or a sale — can happen.

What we don't cover is tree felling, stump removal, or hazardous chemical disposal — if your job involves any of that alongside a garden clear-out, let us know and we can talk through what's within scope.

Responsible Disposal

Green waste is genuinely recyclable, and we treat it that way — composted or recycled wherever possible rather than sent to general landfill. We're a fully insured waste carrier, and aim for an average recycling rate of up to 95% across everything we clear.

Preparing a Property for Sale or Letting

An overgrown garden is one of the first things a buyer or prospective tenant notices, and one of the easiest to fix before photos or viewings. Kerb appeal doesn't stop at the front door — a garden that's visibly gone wild, or a back yard full of old furniture and debris, undermines the rest of the property no matter how well the inside has been presented. It's often the difference between a listing photo that looks cared-for and one that quietly puts people off before they've even booked a viewing.

We clear gardens as a stand-alone job specifically for this — cutting back, clearing debris, and leaving the outdoor space presentable without needing a full landscaping job. Estate agents and landlords often want this done in a tight window ahead of photography or a first viewing, so if you're working to a date, tell us when you get your quote and we'll do our best to fit around it.

Skip Hire Alternative

For a lot of garden clean-ups, hiring a skip means applying for a permit if it's going on the road, waiting for it to arrive, having it sit outside the house for days while it fills, and doing all the loading yourself — including anything heavy, wet, or awkward. We do the loading and take everything away in one visit, sorted for recycling rather than dumped as mixed waste.

We won't claim we're always cheaper than a skip — for a large volume of light, easy-to-load green waste and enough time to do it yourself, a skip can work out less expensive. What we offer is convenience: no permit to arrange, nothing sitting outside your house for days, and no lifting done by you. For most people juggling a garden clear-out around everything else, that trade-off is worth more than a few euro saved. If you want a fuller comparison, our skip hire alternative post goes through the permit, timing, and loading differences in more detail.

What Affects the Cost

We don't publish a fixed price list for garden clearance, because two gardens of similar size can be very different jobs depending on what's actually in them. The main factors we take into account are:

  • Volume — how much needs clearing, not just the size of the garden. A small garden that's been neglected for years can hold more waste than a larger one that's had occasional upkeep.
  • Access to the garden — whether it's reached through a side gate, a narrow passage, or only via the house itself. Easy access generally means a quicker, more straightforward job.
  • Structures involved — old decking, fencing, or other garden structures being removed take more time and vehicle space than loose green waste.
  • How the waste is presented — cuttings and branches already piled up clear faster than a garden where everything still needs to be pulled back and gathered first.

Describe the garden and roughly what's involved when you get in touch, and we'll give you a free, no-obligation quote based on the actual job rather than a generic rate.
