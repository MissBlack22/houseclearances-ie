Garage Clearance

For a lot of Dublin homes, the garage stopped being for the car a long time ago. It becomes the default storage for anything that doesn't have another home — boxes from the last move, furniture waiting for "somewhere to put it," tools, sports gear, and things nobody's quite ready to throw out. HouseClearances.ie clears garages back to empty, usable space — whether that means fitting a car in again, setting up a workshop, or just getting the clutter gone.

What We Typically Clear

No two garages look the same, but the contents tend to fall into a few recognisable groups:

  • Furniture stored "temporarily" for years — an old sofa, spare bed frame, or wardrobe that got moved out of the house and never moved on
  • Tools, DIY equipment, and workbenches — power tools, hand tools, fixings, offcuts of timber, and the workbench itself if it's coming out too
  • Boxes of household overflow from previous moves — the ones that got stacked in the corner "for now" and are still there years later
  • Garden equipment and machinery — lawnmowers, strimmers, hedge trimmers, and the fuel cans and spare parts that go with them
  • Sports and hobby equipment no longer used — bikes, golf clubs, camping gear, kids' outgrown sports kit
  • General accumulated clutter — old paint tins, hardware, extension leads, half-used bags of cement, and all the odds and ends that migrate to a garage because there's nowhere else to put them

If a household is clearing a garage that's also doing double duty as informal storage for a relative or a previous tenant's belongings, that's routine too — just flag it when you enquire so we know what's actually leaving with us.

Reclaiming the Space

A garage clearance is often about more than getting rid of stuff — it's about getting a room back. The reason behind it varies more than people expect:

  • Parking a car under cover again — the single most common reason we get called, especially heading into winter
  • Setting up a home gym — a garage is often the only room in the house with enough floor space for equipment
  • Building a proper workshop — clearing out the general clutter so the space can be used for what it was actually meant for
  • Decluttering ahead of a house move or sale — an empty, clean garage shows far better than one stacked to the rafters, and it's one less thing to deal with after completion
  • Simply not dreading opening the door — sometimes the goal is just to stop the garage being the place nothing good happens

Whatever the reason, we clear the contents completely and leave the space swept and empty, ready for whatever comes next.

Awkward and Heavy Items

Garages tend to collect the things that are hardest to move — old appliances (washing machines, tumble dryers, fridges that got replaced and never got taken away), heavy furniture, solid workbenches bolted or wedged into a corner, and general machinery. These are handled as standard, not treated as a separate, extra-cost job or something we need to send a special crew back for. A workbench built into the space, an old chest freezer, or a stack of paving slabs someone never got round to laying is exactly the kind of thing garage clearances involve — it's not unusual, it's the job. If there's anything unusually large or heavy, mention it when you get your quote so we can plan the right approach, equipment, and crew size for the day.

What Affects the Cost

Every garage clearance is quoted individually rather than off a flat rate, because the same size garage can be a quick job or a much bigger one depending on what's actually in it. The main factors we take into account:

  • Volume — how full the garage actually is, from a few items along one wall to floor-to-ceiling on every side
  • Weight and bulk — a garage full of boxes clears differently from one with a workbench, old appliances, or paving slabs and building materials
  • Access — whether it's an attached garage opening straight onto a driveway, a detached one at the back of a property, or something with tighter access that takes longer to load from
  • Time required — most garages clear in a single visit, but a very full or awkward one may need more time on site or an extra pair of hands

Describe roughly what's in there and how the garage is accessed when you get in touch, and we'll give you a free, accurate quote before any work starts — no guessing, and no surprise costs on the day.

Responsible Disposal

We sort everything as we clear: usable tools, furniture and equipment are set aside for donation or resale, recyclable materials are separated out, and only genuine waste goes to a licensed transfer station. We're a fully insured waste carrier, and aim for an average recycling rate of up to 95% across the items we handle.

Related Services

Garages rarely get cleared in isolation — they tend to hold the overflow from other parts of the house, so it's worth thinking about what else might need doing at the same time:

  • Shed clearance — for the garden side of the same storage problem
  • Attic clearance — another common overflow space in the same house
  • Full house clearance — if the garage is part of a wider clear-out, such as before a move or sale
  • Storage unit clearance — many garages effectively function as an informal storage unit, and if the household also rents an actual unit, we can clear both together

If you're tackling more than one space in the same visit, mention it when you get in touch and we'll quote for all of it together as one job.
