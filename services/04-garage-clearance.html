Garage Clearance

For a lot of Dublin homes, the garage stopped being for the car a long time ago. It becomes the default storage for anything that doesn't have another home — boxes from the last move, furniture waiting for "somewhere to put it," tools, sports gear, and things nobody's quite ready to throw out. HouseClearances.ie clears garages back to empty, usable space — whether that means fitting a car in again, setting up a workshop, or just getting the clutter gone.

What We Typically Clear

  • Furniture stored "temporarily" for years
  • Tools, DIY equipment, and workbenches
  • Boxes of household overflow from previous moves
  • Garden equipment and machinery
  • Sports and hobby equipment no longer used
  • General accumulated clutter — old paint, hardware, odds and ends

Reclaiming the Space

A garage clearance is often about more than getting rid of stuff — it's about getting a room back. Whether the goal is parking a car under cover again, setting up a home gym or workshop, or simply not dreading opening the door, we clear the contents completely and leave the space swept and empty, ready for whatever comes next.

Awkward and Heavy Items

Garages tend to collect the things that are hardest to move — old appliances, heavy furniture, workbenches, and machinery. These are handled as standard, not treated as a separate, extra-cost job. If there's anything unusually large or heavy, mention it when you get your quote so we can plan the right approach and crew for the day.

Responsible Disposal

We sort everything as we clear: usable tools, furniture and equipment are set aside for donation or resale, recyclable materials are separated out, and only genuine waste goes to a licensed transfer station. We're a fully insured waste carrier, and aim for an average recycling rate of up to 95% across the items we handle.

How It Works

  1. Get a free quote — describe roughly what's in the garage, or send photos if that's easier.
  2. We agree a time that suits you.
  3. We clear it in a single visit wherever the volume allows.
  4. Items are sorted responsibly for reuse, recycling, or disposal.
  5. The garage is left empty and swept, ready to be used again.

Often Cleared Alongside

Garage clearance pairs naturally with shed clearance and attic clearance — if you're tackling more than one storage space in the same visit, let us know and we'll quote for all of it together as one job.
