Bereavement Clearance

Clearing a loved one's home is rarely just a practical task. HouseClearances.ie provides a bereavement clearance service across Dublin built around that reality — unhurried, discreet, and led by what the family needs, not a fixed process we push everyone through.

How We Approach This

There's no single right way to clear a family home after a loss, and we don't assume there is. Some families want everything handled quickly and from a distance; others want to be present, go through belongings themselves first, or set aside specific items before anything else is touched. We take our lead from you.

  • Flexible timing. We work to your timeline, not the other way round — whether that's a quick turnaround for a sale or probate deadline, or no rush at all.
  • Discreet. Our crews carry out the work quietly and respectfully, without treating it as routine.
  • Family-led decisions. Items of sentimental or financial value can be set aside for the family to review before anything is cleared, if that's what you'd prefer.
  • Donation-forward. Where a family would like belongings donated rather than disposed of, we prioritise that — many families find it genuinely helpful that things go to someone who needs them.

Estate and Probate Situations

Bereavement clearances often overlap with probate or estate administration, sometimes coordinated with executors or a solicitor as part of settling the estate. We're happy to liaise directly with whoever is managing the process, and to work to any deadlines tied to a sale or handover of the property.

What We Clear

The full contents of a home — furniture, personal effects, clothing, household items, and anything in the garage, shed or attic. See our main House Clearance page for the broader detail on how a full clearance works; this page covers specifically how we approach it in a bereavement context.

Responsible Disposal

We're a fully insured waste carrier. Items in good condition are set aside for donation or resale wherever the family is comfortable with that, and we aim for an average recycling rate of up to 95% across everything we clear. What's left after reuse and recycling is disposed of through a licensed waste transfer station.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we go through the house ourselves before you clear it?

Yes — take whatever time you need. We're happy to arrange the clearance for whenever you're ready, and to set aside anything you'd like to look through first.

Can you work with a solicitor or executor managing the estate?

Yes, we regularly coordinate directly with whoever is handling the process, on timing, access, and any specific instructions for the property.

Can items be donated rather than disposed of?

Yes — if that matters to your family, let us know and we'll prioritise donation for anything in suitable condition.

Is there a rush to book this quickly?

No. Get in touch whenever you're ready — we'll work to your timeline, not the other way round.
