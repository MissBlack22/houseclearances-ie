Storage Unit Clearance

Self-storage is meant to be temporary, but a lot of units end up being paid for month after month long after anyone remembers exactly what's in them. HouseClearances.ie clears storage units across Dublin quickly, whether you're closing an account for good, downsizing what you keep, or clearing a unit on behalf of a family member.

Who This Is For

  • Anyone closing a storage account and wanting the unit emptied in one visit rather than several trips.
  • People downsizing who no longer need everything they've had in storage.
  • Families clearing a unit that belonged to a relative, often alongside a wider bereavement clearance.
  • Businesses clearing stock or equipment from a commercial storage unit.

Facility Access

Storage facilities usually have their own access rules — opening hours, loading-bay booking systems, lift or trolley requirements, and sometimes ID checks at the gate. Tell us which facility you're using and we'll work our schedule around their access hours rather than turning up outside them.

Sized to the Unit

Storage units are a genuinely useful way to scope a job — a 25 sq ft unit and a 100 sq ft unit are very different jobs, and telling us the unit size when you get in touch gets you a much more accurate quote without needing a site visit first.

What We Clear

Household furniture and belongings, boxes, business stock and equipment, and general accumulated contents. As with any clearance, items in good condition are set aside for donation or resale where possible rather than disposed of automatically.

Responsible Disposal

We're a fully insured waste carrier and aim for an average recycling rate of up to 95% across everything we clear — reuse and donation first, recycling second, and only genuine waste going to a licensed transfer station.

How It Works

  1. Get a free quote — tell us the unit size and roughly what's inside.
  2. We agree a time that fits the facility's access hours.
  3. We clear the unit in a single visit wherever possible.
  4. Items are sorted responsibly for reuse, recycling, or disposal.
  5. The unit is left empty, ready to hand back to the facility or account for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need to see the unit before quoting?

Usually not — the unit size (as listed by your storage provider) plus a rough description of contents is normally enough for an accurate quote.

Can you work around the facility's access hours?

Yes — tell us which facility you're using and we'll schedule around their opening hours and any booking requirements.

Can you clear a unit on behalf of someone else, like a relative's account?

Yes, this is common — just make sure you have the necessary access/authorisation from the facility, and we'll take it from there.
