What Counts as Junk?

In practice, "junk removal" and "house clearance" cover a lot of the same ground — old furniture, broken appliances, general household rubbish, garden waste, and the accumulated clutter that builds up in attics, sheds and garages. The difference is really about scale and framing: junk removal often means a single item or a specific space (a shed that's finally getting sorted, an old sofa nobody wants), while a full house clearance covers an entire property. We do both, and we quote based on what's actually there rather than which word you searched.

How Much Does House Clearance Cost in Ireland?

There's no fixed price list, because a single-item junk removal and a full house clearance are very different jobs. The real cost factors are the volume of items, how many rooms or spaces are involved, access and stairs, distance to the loading point, how many staff and vehicles the job needs, and whether sorting has been specifically requested. We won't invent a headline "starting from" price that doesn't reflect the actual job — the accurate way to get a number is a free quote, based on photos or a quick description of what needs clearing.
