# HouseClearances.ie Brand Guidelines

## Brand
**CLEAR • REMOVE • RECYCLE**

## Palette
- Deep Navy `#102A43` — authority/navigation
- Recycling Green `#2E7D32` — CTAs/sustainability
- Accent Orange `#F59E0B` — highlights
- Soft Cream `#F7F8F5` — backgrounds
- Ink `#17212B` — body text
- Muted `#667085` — secondary text
- Border `#D9E0E6`

## Typography
Inter, with system-ui fallback. H1 48–78px/800; H2 36–52px/800; body 16–20px; buttons 14–16px/800.

## Logo
Use the supplied SVG variants. Keep clear space around the mark. Never stretch, rotate or add effects.

## Photography
Prioritise genuine company/vehicle/job photography. Never present generated imagery as a real job.
