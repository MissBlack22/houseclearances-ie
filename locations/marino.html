House Clearance in Marino

Marino is one of Dublin's earliest examples of planned suburban housing — a Dublin Corporation scheme built in the 1920s and 30s, laid out as a series of curving, tree-lined crescents and cul-de-sacs of pebble-dashed semi-detached cottages rather than the grid of terraces found closer to the city. House Clearance Marino jobs tend to follow that pattern: compact gardens, modest box-room attics, and the odd shed or outbuilding, alongside pockets of later infill housing built in among the original scheme over the decades.

House Clearance Services in Marino

Because the original cottages are smaller and more uniform than a lot of Dublin's Victorian housing stock, clearances here are often quicker and more contained — a full cottage clearance, or a garden shed and attic cleared alongside it. The crescent layout means some roads are narrower with limited turning space, so we always ask about access and on-street parking before quoting rather than assuming a standard driveway.

What We Can Clear

We handle full and partial clearances of Marino's cottages and the newer housing built in among them, garden sheds and small outbuildings, box-room and eaves-storage attic spaces, apartment and flat clearances, and bereavement or estate clearances where a family member's home needs to be cleared and handed back.

Relevant Specialist Services
