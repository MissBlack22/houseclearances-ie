Landlord Clearance Checklist: Clearing a Rental Between Tenants

Every day a rental sits uncleared is a day it is not earning. This checklist covers the practical side of clearing a rental property between tenants, whether you are a landlord with one property or an agent with many. Tick items off as you go (your ticks are saved on this device), print it, or save it as a PDF.

This covers the practical side only. The legal position on a tenant's belongings and deposits depends on the circumstances, so take advice or check with the Residential Tenancies Board where there is any doubt.

1. When You Know the Tenancy Is Ending

  • Note the date the tenant is due to leave and the date the keys are due back.
  • Note the date the next tenant is due to move in, or the viewing dates.
  • Check the inventory or condition report from the start of the tenancy.
  • Arrange a final inspection with the tenant, if possible.

2. At the Final Inspection

  • Photograph every room, with the date, before anything changes.
  • Note any belongings left behind, and photograph them.
  • Note any damage, and compare it with the start-of-tenancy record.
  • Get the keys, and note how many have been returned.
  • Take meter readings.
  • Take advice, or check with the Residential Tenancies Board, before disposing of anything a tenant has left behind.

3. Arranging the Clearance

  • Send photos of each room and any stairs, lift or parking to a clearance company for a free quote.
  • Give the date the property must be ready.
  • Ask for the waste collection permit number and proof of insurance.
  • Arrange keys and access with the agent, caretaker or building manager.
  • If it is an apartment, ask the management company about lift booking and loading rules.
  • Agree what happens to anything of value.

4. After the Clearance

  • Check each room has been left swept and tidy.
  • Check any attic, shed, garage or storage area attached to the property.
  • Arrange any cleaning, repairs, painting or a compliance inspection that comes next.
  • Arrange photographs for the new listing.
  • Keep the quote and invoice for your records.

5. Multiple Properties

  • Keep one set of instructions for every clearance: who holds keys, what "ready to re-list" means, who to contact.
  • Ask about a standing arrangement with a single point of contact.
  • Keep a record of each property, the date and the work done.
